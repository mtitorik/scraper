### 3. WATERFALL MULTI-WEBSITE SEARCH & VARIANT SCRAPING
1. **Hierarchical Fallback Order:**
   - **Pass 1 (Website 1):** Search Website 1 for all products. Extract data, download images, run Gemini, and save to the new `.xlsx`. If zero exact matches are found, skip the product for now.
   - **Pass 2 (Website 2):** Search Website 2 **only** for products skipped in Pass 1.
   - **Pass 3 & 4 (Website 3 & Website 4):** Repeat in priority order for remaining unmatched products.
   - **After Final Link:** Any product not found on any site gets `"not found"` in its fields and `Status = "Not Found"` in the output `.xlsx`.
2. **Search Matching & Official/Unofficial Detection:**
   - Open the target homepage in the visible Incognito browser, enter the product name in the search bar, and filter out unrelated accessories unless explicitly part of the product name.
   - Detect and extract **both** Official and Unofficial options whether they appear as **two separate product listings** on the search results page or as **variant buttons** inside a single product page.
   - Classify `"Apple Authorized"` (or explicit `"Official"`) as **Official**, and `"Apple Unauthorized"` (or explicit `"Unofficial"`) as **Unofficial**. Ignore region labels (e.g., USA, SG, UAE, Global) for Official/Unofficial classification.
3. **Variant Click-Loop (Including Out-of-Stock) & Image Naming:**
   - Click through every color, storage capacity, and official/unofficial combination—including **out-of-stock** variants—waiting briefly for prices and images to update.
   - Save high-resolution images inside `[Root Folder]/[Sanitized Product Name]/` named as:
     `{Product_Name}_{Variant_Type}_{Storage}_{Color}.{ext}` (sanitized for Windows and macOS paths).
